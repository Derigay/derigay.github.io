LAND DEVELOPMENT
From Approval to Construction
Successful development requires more than obtaining approvals. It requires a team that understands how planning, design, approvals, budgeting, and construction come together. With experience in both development and construction, Derigay Developments can help identify constructability considerations early, coordinate the required professionals, and provide a seamless transition from approved plans to construction.

Land Development Services Incluede:

1. Site Plan Approval

Coordination and support through the site plan approval process, including working with municipal staff, consultants, and project stakeholders.

2. Minor Variances

Assistance with applications for minor variances where proposed development requires relief from applicable zoning requirements.

3. Zoning & Planning Approvals

Support in navigating municipal zoning requirements, planning policies, and development regulations.

4. Development Applications

Coordination of the application process and required documentation to help move development proposals through municipal review.

5. Pre-Consultation & Municipal Coordination

Working with municipalities and project consultants to identify requirements, approvals, and potential challenges early in the development process.

6. Site Servicing & Infrastructure Coordination

Coordination of civil, servicing, grading, drainage, stormwater management, and other site infrastructure requirements.

7. Consultant Coordination

Working alongside planners, engineers, architects, surveyors, and other professionals to ensure the various components of a development are coordinated effectively.

8. Construction & Project Management

Once approvals are in place, our construction team can carry the project through site development and construction, providing continuity from planning through completion.
